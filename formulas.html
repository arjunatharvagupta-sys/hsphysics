Kinematics

v = v₀ + at x = x₀ + v₀t + ½at² v² = v₀² + 2aΔx x = x₀ + ½(v₀ + v)t

Forces & Dynamics

ΣF = ma W = mg fₛ ≤ μₛN    fₖ = μₖN

Energy & Work

W = Fd cosθ KE = ½mv² PE_g = mgh    PE_s = ½kx² P = W/t = Fv

Momentum

p = mv J = FΔt = Δp Σpᵢ = Σp_f (isolated)

Circular Motion & Gravity

a_c = v²/r F_c = mv²/r F_g = Gm₁m₂/r² v_orbit = √(GM/r) T² ∝ r³

Rotation

ω = ω₀ + αt θ = θ₀ + ω₀t + ½αt² τ = rF sinθ Στ = Iα KE_rot = ½Iω² L = Iω

Waves & Sound

v = fλ f_n = n v / (2L) (string) β = 10 log(I/I₀)

Electrostatics

F = k|q₁q₂|/r² E = kq/r² V = kq/r U = kq₁q₂/r

Circuits

V = IR R_eq series = ΣR 1/R_eq parallel = Σ(1/R) P = IV = I²R = V²/R

Magnetism

F = qvB sinθ F = ILB sinθ ε = −dΦ_B/dt Φ_B = BA cosθ

Optics

1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ n₁ sinθ₁ = n₂ sinθ₂ d sinθ = mλ (interference)

Modern Physics

E = hf = hc/λ K_max = hf − φ λ = h/p E = mc² γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²)
