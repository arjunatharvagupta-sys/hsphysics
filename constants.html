Physical Constants & Data

Values commonly used in high school and intro college physics.

Fundamental

c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² g ≈ 9.80 m/s² k = 9.00 × 10⁹ N·m²/C² ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C²/(N·m²) μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s e = 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ C k_B = 1.38 × 10⁻²³ J/K N_A = 6.02 × 10²³ /mol R = 8.31 J/(mol·K)

Particle Masses

m_e = 9.11 × 10⁻³¹ kg m_p = 1.67 × 10⁻²⁷ kg m_n ≈ 1.67 × 10⁻²⁷ kg 1 u = 1.66 × 10⁻²⁷ kg = 931.5 MeV/c²

Other Useful

1 eV = 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ J 1 atm = 1.01 × 10⁵ Pa v_sound (air, 20°C) ≈ 343 m/s I₀ (hearing) = 10⁻¹² W/m² hc ≈ 1240 eV·nm

Earth / Astronomy (approx.)

M_Earth = 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg R_Earth = 6.37 × 10⁶ m M_Sun = 1.99 × 10³⁰ kg AU ≈ 1.50 × 10¹¹ m
