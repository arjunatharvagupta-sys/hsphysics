Graphs and Linearization

Common linearizations

Period vs √L for pendulum → slope = 2π/√g.
T² vs m for mass-spring → slope = 4π²/k.
1/dᵢ vs 1/dₒ for lenses → intercepts relate to 1/f.

Uncertainty and Error Bars

Lab Report Skeleton

1. Purpose / question
2. Theory (equation you will test)
3. Apparatus and procedure (enough to replicate)
4. Data table with units and uncertainties
5. Graph with labels, units, best-fit line
6. Calculation of result from slope/intercept
7. Comparison to expected value; % error
8. Sources of uncertainty and improvements

Classic HS Labs (ideas)

• g from free fall or pendulum
• μ from sliding block or Atwood with friction
• k from mass-spring period or static stretch
• Conservation of momentum (air track / video)
• Specific heat by calorimetry
• Resistance of a wire vs length/thickness
• Focal length of a lens
• Speed of sound from closed-tube resonance
