Oscillations & Simple Harmonic Motion

Repeating motion about an equilibrium — springs, pendulums, period, frequency, and energy exchange.

What is Simple Harmonic Motion?

Period and Frequency

T = 2π √(m/k)    (mass-spring) T = 2π √(L/g)    (simple pendulum, small θ) f = 1/T    ω = 2πf

Tip

Heavier mass or weaker spring → longer period. Longer pendulum → longer period. g larger (stronger gravity) → shorter pendulum period.

Pendulums

Common mistake

Using the pendulum formula for large angles or for a physical pendulum without adjusting the effective length. Also forgetting that mass cancels — period does not depend on the bob’s mass.
