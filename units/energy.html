04 — Work, Energy & Power

Energy is the capacity to do work. Work transfers energy. Power is how fast that transfer happens.

Work

ELI5

Work is what happens when a force makes something move in the direction of that force. If you push a box and it slides, you did work. If you push and it doesn’t move, you did zero work.

High School

Work done by a constant force is W = F·d·cosθ, where θ is the angle between the force and the displacement. Only the component of force parallel to the motion does work. Unit: joule (J) = N·m. Work can be positive (energy added) or negative (energy removed).

Professional

The infinitesimal work done by a force is the scalar product dW = F · dr. For a finite displacement the work is the line integral ∫ F · dr along the path. When the force is constant this reduces to W = F · Δr = Fd cosθ. Work is a scalar and frame-dependent; it is the energy transferred by the force across the system boundary.

W = Fd cosθ W = ∫ F · dr (variable force)

Kinetic Energy

ELI5

Kinetic energy is the energy something has because it’s moving. Faster or heavier means more kinetic energy.

High School

KE = ½mv². It is always positive and depends on the square of speed. The work–energy theorem states that the net work done on an object equals its change in kinetic energy: W_net = ΔKE.

Professional

The kinetic energy of a particle is the scalar ½mv² derived from the work–energy theorem by integrating F = ma along the trajectory. For a system of particles the total kinetic energy separates into center-of-mass and relative contributions. It is the quadratic form associated with the metric of velocity space in classical mechanics.

KE = ½mv² W_net = ΔKE

Potential Energy

ELI5

Potential energy is stored energy waiting to be turned into motion. A book on a high shelf has gravitational potential energy; a stretched spring has elastic potential energy.

High School

Gravitational PE near Earth: PE_g = mgh (h measured from a chosen zero). Elastic PE of a spring: PE_s = ½kx². Potential energy is defined only for conservative forces and is a property of the system configuration.

Professional

A force is conservative if its work is path-independent (equivalently, if ∇ × F = 0). For such forces a scalar potential energy function U(r) exists such that F = −∇U. The change in potential energy equals the negative of the work done by the conservative force. Absolute values of U are arbitrary; only differences are physically meaningful.

PE_g = mgh PE_s = ½kx²

Conservation of Mechanical Energy

ELI5

If no friction or other “lossy” forces are around, the total of kinetic plus potential energy stays the same. Energy just changes forms — up high and slow becomes low and fast.

High School

When only conservative forces do work, mechanical energy is conserved: KEᵢ + PEᵢ = KE_f + PE_f. Non-conservative work (friction, applied forces) changes the mechanical energy: W_nc = ΔME.

Professional

In a closed system subject only to conservative internal forces, the total mechanical energy E = T + U is a constant of the motion. When non-conservative forces are present, the work they perform equals the change in mechanical energy. This is a direct integral consequence of Newton’s second law for time-independent potentials.

KEᵢ + PEᵢ = KE_f + PE_f    (conservative) W_nc = Δ(KE + PE)

Power

ELI5

Power is how fast you do work or use energy. A powerful engine can do the same work in less time.

High School

Average power P = W/Δt = ΔE/Δt. Instantaneous power P = F·v. Unit: watt (W) = J/s.

Professional

Power is the time derivative of work (or energy transfer): P = dW/dt. For a force acting on a moving point it is the scalar product F · v. It quantifies the rate of energy conversion or transfer and is a scalar.

P = W/t P = Fv cosθ
