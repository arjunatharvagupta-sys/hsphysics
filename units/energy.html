Work, Energy & Power

Energy is the capacity to do work. Work transfers energy. Power is the rate of that transfer.

Work

W = Fd cosθ W = ∫ F · dr

Kinetic Energy

KE = ½mv² W_net = ΔKE

Potential Energy

PE_g = mgh PE_s = ½kx²

Conservation of Mechanical Energy

KEᵢ + PEᵢ = KE_f + PE_f W_nc = Δ(KE + PE)

Power

P = W/t P = Fv cosθ
