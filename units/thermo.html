Thermodynamics

Temperature, heat, ideal gases, the first and second laws — core AP Physics 2 thermal topics.

Temperature and Kinetic Theory

KE_avg = (3/2) kT    (per molecule) T_K = T_°C + 273

Ideal Gas Law

PV = nRT PV = NkT

Heat and Internal Energy

Q = mcΔT ΔU = nC_v ΔT    (ideal gas)

First Law of Thermodynamics

ΔU = Q − W    (W by system) W = PΔV    (isobaric)

Tip

Always confirm the sign convention used in your course. Some texts write ΔU = Q + W with W the work done on the system.

Second Law and Entropy (Brief)

η_Carnot = 1 − T_C / T_H
