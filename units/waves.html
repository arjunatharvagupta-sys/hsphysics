Waves & Sound

Wave properties, superposition, standing waves, sound, Doppler, and intensity.

Wave Basics

v = fλ T = 1/f

Speed on a String

v = √(T/μ)

Interference and Standing Waves

String: f_n = n v / (2L) Closed pipe: f = n v / (4L)   (n odd)

Common mistake

Using the same harmonic formula for open and closed pipes. Closed-open pipes support only odd harmonics.

Sound and Intensity

v_sound ≈ 343 m/s β = 10 log(I/I₀)

Doppler Effect

f' = f (v ± v_o) / (v ± v_s)

Tip

Numerator: observer. Denominator: source. Toward → sign that raises f'; away → sign that lowers f.
