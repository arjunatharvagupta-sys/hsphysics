08 — Waves & Sound

How energy travels through oscillations: wavelength, frequency, interference, standing waves, and sound.

What is a Wave?

ELI5

A wave is a traveling disturbance that carries energy without permanently moving the material itself. Think of a ripple on a pond: the water mostly goes up and down while the ripple moves outward.

High School

A wave is a periodic disturbance that transfers energy through a medium or space. Mechanical waves need a medium; electromagnetic waves do not. Transverse waves oscillate perpendicular to the direction of travel; longitudinal waves oscillate parallel to it.

Professional

A wave is a solution of a hyperbolic partial differential equation (the wave equation) that propagates a disturbance of a field quantity through space and time. Mechanical waves are solutions of the continuum equations of a material medium; electromagnetic waves are solutions of Maxwell’s equations in vacuum or media.

Wave Properties

ELI5

Wavelength is the length of one full wiggle. Frequency is how many wiggles pass by each second. Amplitude is how tall the wiggle is. Wave speed is how fast the wiggle pattern travels.

High School

Wavelength λ = distance between successive crests. Frequency f = number of cycles per second (Hz). Period T = 1/f. Amplitude A = maximum displacement from equilibrium. Wave speed v = fλ.

Professional

A monochromatic traveling wave may be written y(x,t) = A cos(kx − ωt + φ), where k = 2π/λ is the wave number and ω = 2πf is the angular frequency. The phase speed is v = ω/k = fλ. Energy density and intensity scale with the square of amplitude.

v = fλ T = 1/f k = 2π/λ    ω = 2πf

Interference & Standing Waves

ELI5

When two waves meet, their heights add. If crests meet crests you get a bigger wave (constructive). If crest meets trough they cancel (destructive). Standing waves are the special patterns that stay put when waves bounce back and forth on a string or in a tube.

High School

Superposition: the net displacement is the sum of individual displacements. Constructive interference occurs when path difference = mλ; destructive when = (m+½)λ. Standing waves on a string fixed at both ends have wavelengths λ = 2L/n and frequencies f_n = n v/(2L).

Professional

Linearity of the wave equation implies the superposition principle. Boundary conditions quantize the allowed modes of a finite system, producing discrete eigenfrequencies and eigenfunctions (standing waves). Nodes are locations of permanent destructive interference; antinodes of permanent constructive interference.

Standing wave (string): f_n = n v / (2L) λ_n = 2L / n

Sound

ELI5

Sound is a longitudinal wave of pressure and density variations traveling through air (or another medium). Higher frequency means higher pitch; larger amplitude means louder sound.

High School

Sound is a longitudinal mechanical wave. Speed in air at 20 °C ≈ 343 m/s. Intensity I ∝ A². Decibel level β = 10 log(I/I₀). Doppler effect: frequency shifts when source or observer moves relative to the medium.

Professional

Sound waves are adiabatic compressional disturbances in a fluid. The speed is c = √(B/ρ) or √(γP/ρ) for an ideal gas. The Doppler formula for frequency follows from the invariance of phase and the relative motion of source, observer, and medium. Intensity is the time-averaged power per unit area carried by the wave.

v_sound ≈ 343 m/s (air, 20 °C) β = 10 log(I/I₀) f' = f (v ± v_o)/(v ± v_s) (Doppler)
