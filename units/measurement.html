Measurement & Uncertainty

Significant figures, accuracy vs precision, uncertainty, SI units, and dimensional analysis — the habits that keep answers honest.

Significant Figures

× or ÷ → fewest sig figs + or − → least precise decimal place

Example

12.50 cm × 3.2 cm → 40 cm² (2 sig figs). 12.50 + 3.2 → 15.7 cm (tenth place).

Accuracy vs Precision

Uncertainty and Percent Error

% uncertainty = (Δx/x)×100% % error = |(x_exp − x_true)/x_true|×100%

Lab tip

Report measurements as value ± uncertainty with matching decimals (e.g. 12.4 ± 0.2 cm). Never give more precision than the instrument allows.

SI Units and Prefixes

1 N = 1 kg·m/s² 1 J = 1 N·m = 1 kg·m²/s² 1 W = 1 J/s

Dimensional Analysis

Habit

Carry units through every step. If the final unit is wrong, an algebra or conversion error occurred. Limiting cases (v → 0, m → ∞, …) catch many formula mistakes.
