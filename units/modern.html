13 — Modern Physics

Brief introduction to the 20th-century ideas that changed physics: relativity, photons, and atoms.

Special Relativity (Basics)

ELI5

Nothing can go faster than light. When things move very fast, time slows down for them and lengths shrink — but only from the point of view of someone watching from outside.

High School

Two postulates: (1) laws of physics are the same in all inertial frames, (2) speed of light c is the same for all observers. Consequences: time dilation Δt = γ Δt₀, length contraction L = L₀/γ, where γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²). Mass–energy equivalence: E = mc².

Professional

Special relativity is the geometry of Minkowski spacetime. The Lorentz transformations leave the interval ds² = −c²dt² + dx² + dy² + dz² invariant. Time dilation and length contraction are direct consequences of that invariance. The energy–momentum four-vector yields E² = (pc)² + (mc²)² and the rest energy mc².

γ = 1 / √(1 − v²/c²) Δt = γ Δt₀ E = mc²

Photons & Photoelectric Effect

ELI5

Light comes in tiny packets called photons. Each photon carries a fixed amount of energy that depends only on its color (frequency). That is why some colors of light can knock electrons out of metal while others cannot, no matter how bright they are.

High School

Photon energy E = hf = hc/λ. Photoelectric effect: electrons are emitted only if f > f₀ (threshold). Kinetic energy of electrons K_max = hf − φ, where φ is the work function. Intensity affects number of electrons, not their individual energies.

Professional

Einstein’s photon hypothesis explains the photoelectric effect by treating light as quanta of energy hf. The work function is the minimum energy needed to liberate an electron from the metal surface. The linear relation between K_max and frequency yields a direct measurement of Planck’s constant.

E = hf = hc / λ K_max = hf − φ

Wave–Particle Duality & Bohr Model

ELI5

Tiny particles like electrons can act like waves. Atoms have only certain allowed energy levels — electrons can jump between them by absorbing or emitting a photon of exactly the right energy.

High School

de Broglie wavelength λ = h/p. Bohr model: electrons orbit in quantized angular-momentum states; energy levels E_n = −13.6 eV / n² for hydrogen. Photons are emitted or absorbed when electrons change levels: ΔE = hf.

Professional

de Broglie’s relation λ = h/p unifies the wave and particle aspects of matter. The Bohr model was a semi-classical precursor to quantum mechanics that correctly predicted the hydrogen spectrum by quantizing angular momentum. Full quantum mechanics replaces orbits with stationary-state wave functions and energy eigenvalues of the Schrödinger equation.

λ = h / p E_n = −13.6 eV / n² (hydrogen) ΔE = hf
