Momentum & Collisions

Momentum is mass in motion. It is conserved in isolated systems and is the key to analyzing collisions and explosions.

Linear Momentum

p = mv

Impulse

J = FΔt = Δp

Conservation of Momentum

Σ m vᵢ = Σ m v_f

Elastic and Inelastic Collisions

Elastic: KE and p both conserved Perfectly inelastic: m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ = (m₁+m₂)v_f
