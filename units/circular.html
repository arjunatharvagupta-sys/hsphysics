Circular Motion & Gravity

Centripetal acceleration, the real forces that provide it, orbital motion, and universal gravitation.

Centripetal Acceleration and Force

a_c = v²/r = ω²r F_c = mv²/r v = 2πr/T    ω = 2π/T

Common misconception

Drawing a “centripetal force” arrow in addition to tension/gravity/friction on a free-body diagram. Identify the real forces; their radial component is the centripetal force.

Horizontal Circles and Banked Curves

v = √(rg tanθ)    (banked, no friction)

Vertical Circles

v_min (top) = √(gr)

Universal Gravitation

F_g = G m₁ m₂ / r² g = GM / R²

Orbits and Kepler

v_orbit = √(GM/r) T² = (4π²/GM) r³

Tip

Escape speed from the surface is √(2GM/R) = √(2gR). It is √2 times circular-orbit speed at the surface.
