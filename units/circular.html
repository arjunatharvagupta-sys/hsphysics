Circular Motion & Gravity

Moving in circles requires a center-pointing force. Gravity supplies that force for planets and satellites.

Uniform Circular Motion

a_c = v²/r = ω²r F_c = mv²/r v = 2πr/T

Universal Gravitation

F_g = G m₁ m₂ / r² G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²

Orbits

v_orbit = √(GM/r) T² = (4π²/GM) r³
