Kinematics

Describing motion without asking what causes it — position, velocity, acceleration, and the equations that connect them.

Distance and Displacement

Speed and Velocity

v_avg = Δx / Δt speed_avg = total distance / total time v = dx/dt

Acceleration

a_avg = Δv / Δt a = dv/dt = d²x/dt²

Constant-Acceleration Equations

v = v₀ + at x = x₀ + v₀t + ½at² v² = v₀² + 2a(x − x₀) x = x₀ + ½(v₀ + v)t

Example

A car starts from rest and accelerates at 3.0 m/s² for 5.0 s. Distance traveled: x = ½(3.0)(5.0)² = 37.5 m ≈ 38 m (two significant figures).

Projectile Motion
