07 — Rotational Motion

Everything that spins: angular versions of position, velocity, acceleration, force, mass, and momentum.

Angular Displacement, Velocity, Acceleration

ELI5

Instead of how far something moves in a straight line, we now care about how many degrees or radians it has turned. Angular velocity is how fast it is spinning; angular acceleration is how quickly that spin rate is changing.

High School

Angular displacement θ is measured in radians. Average angular velocity ω = Δθ/Δt. Average angular acceleration α = Δω/Δt. The kinematic equations for constant α are exact analogs of the linear ones: ω = ω₀ + αt, θ = θ₀ + ω₀t + ½αt², etc.

Professional

Angular displacement is the oriented angle through which a rigid body rotates about a fixed axis. Angular velocity ω = dθ/dt and angular acceleration α = dω/dt are axial vectors. For constant α the integrated kinematic relations mirror the linear constant-acceleration set.

ω = ω₀ + αt θ = θ₀ + ω₀t + ½αt² ω² = ω₀² + 2αΔθ s = rθ    v = rω    a_t = rα

Torque

ELI5

Torque is the rotational version of force. It tells you how effectively a force makes something start or stop spinning. Pushing farther from the hinge or pushing perpendicular gives more torque.

High School

Torque τ = rF sinθ = rF⊥. It is a vector (direction given by right-hand rule). Net torque produces angular acceleration: Στ = Iα. Unit: N·m.

Professional

Torque about a point is the cross product τ = r × F. For a rigid body constrained to rotate about a fixed axis, the component of net torque along that axis equals Iα, where I is the moment of inertia about the axis.

τ = rF sinθ Στ = Iα

Moment of Inertia

ELI5

Moment of inertia is the rotational version of mass. It measures how hard it is to change an object’s spin. Mass farther from the axis makes it harder to spin up.

High School

I = Σmr² for point masses. Continuous objects use standard formulas (e.g., solid cylinder I = ½MR² about central axis). Parallel-axis theorem: I = I_cm + Md².

Professional

The moment of inertia about an axis is the second moment of mass distribution, I = ∫ r_⊥² dm. It is the eigenvalue of the inertia tensor corresponding to that axis. The parallel-axis theorem relates I about any axis parallel to one through the center of mass.

I = Σ m r² I = I_cm + Md²

Rotational Kinetic Energy & Angular Momentum

ELI5

A spinning object stores kinetic energy just because it is spinning. Angular momentum is the “quantity of rotation” and stays constant if no external torques act (ice skater pulling arms in spins faster).

High School

KE_rot = ½Iω². Angular momentum L = Iω. Conservation of angular momentum: if Στ_ext = 0 then L is constant. This is why a collapsing star or a skater speeds up as I decreases.

Professional

Rotational kinetic energy is ½ω · L. Angular momentum about a point is L = r × p for a particle and Iω for a rigid body about a principal axis. When the net external torque about a point vanishes, angular momentum about that point is conserved.

KE_rot = ½Iω² L = Iω Στ_ext = dL/dt
