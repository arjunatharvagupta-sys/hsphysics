Rotational Motion

Angular kinematics, torque, moment of inertia, rotational energy, and angular momentum conservation.

Angular Kinematics

ω = ω₀ + αt θ = θ₀ + ω₀t + ½αt² s = rθ   v = rω   a_t = rα

Torque

τ = rF sinθ Στ = Iα

Tip

For statics, pick an axis through the line of action of an unknown force so its torque is zero and it drops out of the torque equation.

Moment of Inertia

I = Σ m r² I = I_cm + Md²

Rotational Energy and Rolling

KE_rot = ½Iω² Rolling: v = rω

Example

Solid sphere vs hoop down the same incline: sphere (k = ⅖) reaches bottom faster than hoop (k = 1) because more energy goes into translation.

Angular Momentum

L = Iω Στ_ext = dL/dt
