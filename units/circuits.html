10 — Electric Circuits

Current, resistance, voltage, Ohm’s law, series & parallel, and power in circuits.

Current, Voltage, Resistance

ELI5

Current is the flow of electric charge. Voltage is the “push” that makes charge flow. Resistance is how much the material fights against that flow.

High School

Current I = ΔQ/Δt (amperes). Conventional current is the direction positive charges would flow. Voltage (potential difference) is the work per unit charge. Resistance R = V/I. Ohm’s law: V = IR for ohmic materials.

Professional

Current density J = nqv_d; total current I = ∫ J · dA. Ohm’s law in local form is J = σE. Resistance of a conductor is R = ρL/A, where ρ is resistivity. Voltage is the line integral of the electric field.

I = ΔQ / Δt V = IR R = ρL / A

Series & Parallel Circuits

ELI5

Series: one path only — current is the same everywhere; voltages add. Parallel: multiple paths — voltage is the same across each branch; currents add.

High School

Series: I same, V total = ΣV, R_eq = ΣR. Parallel: V same, I total = ΣI, 1/R_eq = Σ(1/R). Real circuits are combinations; reduce step by step to a single equivalent resistance.

Professional

Kirchhoff’s current law (junction rule) expresses charge conservation; Kirchhoff’s voltage law (loop rule) expresses energy conservation (conservative electrostatic field). Series and parallel reduction are the simplest applications of these laws for two-terminal networks of resistors.

Series: R_eq = R₁ + R₂ + … Parallel: 1/R_eq = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + …

Electric Power

ELI5

Power tells you how fast a circuit converts electrical energy into heat, light, or motion. A higher-power device uses energy faster.

High School

P = IV = I²R = V²/R. Energy used E = Pt. Household energy is often measured in kilowatt-hours.

Professional

Instantaneous power delivered to a circuit element is P = IV. For a resistor this becomes I²R or V²/R and appears as Joule heating. The time-averaged power in AC circuits involves the power factor cosφ.

P = IV = I²R = V²/R E = Pt

Kirchhoff’s Rules

ELI5

At any junction, charge doesn’t pile up — what flows in must flow out. Around any closed loop, the total “ups” and “downs” in voltage must cancel to zero.

High School

Junction rule: ΣI_in = ΣI_out. Loop rule: ΣΔV = 0 around any closed loop. Assign current directions, write equations, and solve the resulting system.

Professional

Kirchhoff’s current law is the integral form of charge conservation at a node. Kirchhoff’s voltage law follows from the electrostatic field being irrotational (∮ E · dl = 0). Together they generate a complete set of linear equations for the branch currents in a lumped resistive network.
