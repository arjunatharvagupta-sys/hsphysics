Electric Circuits

Current, resistance, Ohm’s law, series/parallel, Kirchhoff, power, and circuit problem strategy.

Current, Voltage, Resistance

I = ΔQ/Δt V = IR R = ρL/A

Common misconception

Current is “used up” in a resistor. Current is the same through series elements; energy is converted, charge is not consumed.

Series and Parallel

Series: R_eq = R₁ + R₂ + … Parallel: 1/R_eq = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + …

Tip

Two equal resistors in parallel → R/2. Adding a parallel path always decreases R_eq. Adding series always increases R_eq.

Kirchhoff’s Rules

Strategy

1. Label currents and directions. 2. Write one KCL per independent junction. 3. Write KVL for enough loops. 4. Solve. 5. Negative current means opposite direction.

Electric Power

P = IV = I²R = V²/R

Example

120 V across 60 Ω: I = 2 A, P = 240 W. Same resistor at 60 V: P = 60 W (V²/R scales with V²).
