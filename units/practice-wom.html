Waves, Optics & Modern Practice

Try each before reading the approach.

1. Standing Waves on a String

A 1.2 m string fixed at both ends has wave speed 240 m/s. Find the fundamental frequency and the frequency of the 3rd harmonic.

Approach

f₁ = v/(2L) = 240/(2.4) = 100 Hz.
f₃ = 3f₁ = 300 Hz.

2. Doppler

A siren emits 800 Hz. Source moves toward a stationary observer at 20 m/s (v_sound = 340 m/s). Heard frequency?

Approach

f' = f · v/(v − v_s) = 800 × 340/(340 − 20) = 800 × 340/320 = 850 Hz.

3. Snell’s Law

Light in air (n = 1.00) hits glass (n = 1.50) at 40° from the normal. Refracted angle? Critical angle for glass → air?

Approach

sinθ₂ = (1/1.50) sin40° = 0.428 ⇒ θ₂ = 25.4°.
sinθ_c = 1/1.50 ⇒ θ_c = 41.8°.

4. Thin Lens

An object is 30 cm from a converging lens with f = 10 cm. Image distance and magnification?

Approach

1/dᵢ = 1/f − 1/dₒ = 1/10 − 1/30 = 1/15 ⇒ dᵢ = 15 cm (real).
m = −dᵢ/dₒ = −0.50 (inverted, half size).

5. Double Slit

Slits 0.20 mm apart, screen 1.5 m away, λ = 600 nm. Distance from central bright to first bright fringe?

Approach

y = mλL/d = (1)(6×10⁻⁷)(1.5)/(2×10⁻⁴) = 4.5×10⁻³ m = 4.5 mm.

6. Photoelectric

Work function 2.5 eV. Light of wavelength 400 nm. K_max of electrons? Threshold wavelength?

Approach

E = hc/λ ≈ 1240/400 = 3.10 eV.
K_max = 3.10 − 2.5 = 0.60 eV.
λ_th = hc/φ = 1240/2.5 = 496 nm.

7. Bohr Hydrogen

Photon wavelength for n = 3 → n = 2 transition in hydrogen?

Approach

E₃ = −13.6/9 = −1.51 eV. E₂ = −3.40 eV.
ΔE = 1.89 eV. λ = 1240/1.89 ≈ 656 nm (Balmer red).
