09 — Electrostatics

Electric charge at rest: forces, fields, potential, and energy.

Electric Charge

ELI5

Charge is a basic property of particles. There are two kinds: positive and negative. Same kinds push each other away; opposite kinds pull toward each other.

High School

Charge is quantized in units of e = 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ C. Charge is conserved. Conductors allow charge to move freely; insulators do not. Charging methods: friction, conduction, induction.

Professional

Electric charge is a conserved Lorentz scalar. The elementary charge e is a fundamental constant. Macroscopic charge densities are continuous approximations to large numbers of elementary charges. Charge conservation is expressed locally by the continuity equation ∂ρ/∂t + ∇·J = 0.

e = 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁹ C

Coulomb’s Law

ELI5

The electric force between two charges gets stronger when the charges are larger and weaker when they are farther apart — specifically, it drops with the square of the distance.

High School

F = k |q₁ q₂| / r², where k = 9.0 × 10⁹ N·m²/C². Direction is along the line joining the charges: attractive for opposite signs, repulsive for like signs. Force is a vector; use components when multiple charges are present.

Professional

Coulomb’s law gives the electrostatic force between two point charges in free space: F = (1/(4πε₀)) q₁q₂ r̂ / r². It is an inverse-square central force, conservative, and the foundation of classical electrostatics. Superposition holds because Maxwell’s equations are linear.

F = k |q₁q₂| / r² k = 9.0 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²

Electric Field

ELI5

An electric field is the invisible influence that a charge creates in the space around it. Another charge placed in that field feels a force. Field lines show the direction a positive test charge would be pushed.

High School

E = F/q₀ (force on a small positive test charge). For a point charge E = kq / r². Field lines begin on positive charges and end on negative charges; density indicates strength. Uniform fields exist between parallel plates.

Professional

The electric field is defined as the force per unit charge in the limit of a vanishing test charge: E = lim (F/q₀). It is a vector field satisfying ∇·E = ρ/ε₀ and ∇×E = 0 in electrostatics. The field of a point charge is E = (1/(4πε₀)) q r̂ / r².

E = F / q E = kq / r² (point charge)

Electric Potential & Potential Energy

ELI5

Electric potential is like height for gravity — it tells you how much energy a charge would have if you placed it there. Moving a charge “uphill” against the field stores potential energy.

High School

Potential difference V = ΔU/q. For a point charge V = kq/r (taking V=0 at infinity). PE of two point charges U = kq₁q₂/r. In a uniform field ΔV = Ed.

Professional

Electric potential is the scalar potential whose negative gradient is the electric field: E = −∇V. Absolute potential is defined only up to a constant; differences are measurable. The potential energy of a charge configuration is (ε₀/2)∫E² dV or equivalently (1/2)Σ qᵢVᵢ.

V = kq / r U = kq₁q₂ / r ΔV = Ed (uniform field)
