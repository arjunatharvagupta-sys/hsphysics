Magnetism

Magnetic fields, Lorentz force, currents in fields, induction, and Lenz’s law.

Magnetic Fields

Force on a Moving Charge

F = qvB sinθ r = mv/(qB)

Common mistake

Thinking magnetic force can speed up or slow down a charge. It only changes direction. Work is always zero for the magnetic part of the Lorentz force.

Force on a Current-Carrying Wire

F = ILB sinθ

Magnetic Field from Currents

Wire: B = μ₀I/(2πr) Solenoid: B = μ₀ n I

Electromagnetic Induction

ε = −dΦ_B/dt Φ_B = BA cosθ ε = Bℓv

Lenz checklist

1. Is flux increasing or decreasing? 2. What B direction would oppose that change? 3. What current direction produces that B? (Right-hand rule.)
