Mechanics Practice

Worked-style problems. Try each before reading the approach. Answers at the bottom of each block.

1. Kinematics

A car accelerates from rest at 2.5 m/s² for 8.0 s, then coasts at constant speed for 12 s, then brakes at −4.0 m/s² until stopping. Find (a) speed after the acceleration phase, (b) total distance.

Approach

(a) v = at = 2.5×8 = 20 m/s.
(b) Phase 1: x₁ = ½at² = 80 m. Phase 2: x₂ = 20×12 = 240 m. Phase 3: 0 = 20² + 2(−4)x₃ ⇒ x₃ = 50 m. Total = 370 m.

2. Newton’s Laws

A 5.0 kg block on a horizontal surface is pulled by a 30 N force at 25° above horizontal. μₖ = 0.20. Find acceleration.

Approach

N + F sin25° = mg ⇒ N = 49 − 12.7 = 36.3 N.
fₖ = 0.20×36.3 = 7.3 N.
ΣFₓ = 30 cos25° − 7.3 = 19.9 N.
a = 19.9/5.0 = 4.0 m/s².

3. Energy

A 0.50 kg block slides from rest down a frictionless ramp of height 1.8 m, then across a rough patch (μₖ = 0.30) of length 2.0 m. Speed after the rough patch?

Approach

mgh = ½mv² + fₖd = ½mv² + μₖmgd.
gh = ½v² + μₖgd
v² = 2g(h − μₖd) = 2×9.8×(1.8 − 0.60) = 23.5
v = 4.9 m/s.

4. Momentum

A 1.2 kg cart moving at 0.80 m/s collides and sticks with a 0.80 kg cart at rest. Joint speed? KE lost?

Approach

m₁v₁ = (m₁+m₂)v ⇒ v = 1.2×0.80/2.0 = 0.48 m/s.
KEᵢ = ½(1.2)(0.64) = 0.384 J. KE_f = ½(2.0)(0.230) = 0.230 J. Lost = 0.15 J.

5. Circular

A 0.20 kg mass on a 0.50 m string swings in a vertical circle. Minimum speed at the top so the string stays taut? Tension at the bottom if speed there is 4.0 m/s?

Approach

Top critical: mg = mv²/r ⇒ v = √(gr) = √4.9 = 2.2 m/s.
Bottom: T − mg = mv²/r ⇒ T = m(g + v²/r) = 0.20(9.8 + 32) = 8.4 N.

6. Rolling

A solid sphere (I = ⅖MR²) rolls without slipping from rest down a 2.0 m high incline. Speed at the bottom?

Approach

Mgh = ½Mv² + ½Iω² = ½Mv² + ½(⅖MR²)(v/R)² = (7/10)Mv².
v = √(10gh/7) = √(28) = 5.3 m/s.
