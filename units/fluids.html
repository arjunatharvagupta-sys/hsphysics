Fluids

Pressure, buoyancy, continuity, and Bernoulli — the core of AP Physics 1 fluids and standard HS treatment.

Density and Pressure

ρ = m / V P = F / A P = P₀ + ρgh

Buoyancy (Archimedes)

F_b = ρ_fluid V_disp g

Common mistake

Using the object’s volume when it is only partially submerged. Buoyant force depends on the volume of fluid actually displaced, not the object’s total volume unless it is fully submerged.

Continuity

A₁v₁ = A₂v₂

Bernoulli’s Principle

P + ½ρv² + ρgh = constant

Tip

Identify the two points you care about, write Bernoulli between them, and use continuity if areas change. Watch whether height differences matter.
