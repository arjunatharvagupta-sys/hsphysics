Electricity & Magnetism Practice

Try each problem before reading the approach.

1. Coulomb Force

Two charges, +3.0 μC and −2.0 μC, are 0.50 m apart. Find the force on each (magnitude and type).

Approach

F = k|q₁q₂|/r² = (9×10⁹)(3×10⁻⁶)(2×10⁻⁶)/(0.25) = 0.216 N, attractive.

2. Electric Field and Force

A +4.0 nC charge sits in a uniform field E = 500 N/C east. Force on the charge? If mass is 2.0×10⁻⁶ kg and released from rest, acceleration?

Approach

F = qE = 4×10⁻⁹ × 500 = 2.0×10⁻⁶ N east.
a = F/m = 1.0 m/s² east.

3. Series–Parallel Circuit

A 12 V battery drives 4.0 Ω and 6.0 Ω in parallel, that combination in series with 3.0 Ω. Find total current and voltage across the 3.0 Ω resistor.

Approach

Parallel: 1/R_p = 1/4 + 1/6 = 5/12 ⇒ R_p = 2.4 Ω.
R_total = 2.4 + 3.0 = 5.4 Ω.
I_total = 12/5.4 = 2.22 A.
V_3Ω = I×3 = 6.7 V.

4. Power

A 60 W bulb designed for 120 V is connected to 120 V. Current? Resistance? Energy used in 5.0 hours (kWh)?

Approach

I = P/V = 0.50 A. R = V/I = 240 Ω.
E = Pt = 0.060 kW × 5.0 h = 0.30 kWh.

5. Magnetic Force on Charge

A proton (q = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C, m = 1.67×10⁻²⁷ kg) moves at 3.0×10⁵ m/s perpendicular to B = 0.50 T. Find (a) force magnitude, (b) radius of the circular path.

Approach

(a) F = qvB = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ × 3×10⁵ × 0.50 = 2.4×10⁻¹⁴ N.
(b) r = mv/(qB) = (1.67×10⁻²⁷)(3×10⁵)/(1.6×10⁻¹⁹ × 0.50) = 6.3×10⁻³ m.

6. Induction

A coil of 50 turns, area 0.020 m², is in a field that drops from 0.80 T to 0 in 0.10 s (field perpendicular to the coil face). Average induced emf?

Approach

Φ_i = BA = 0.016 Wb. Φ_f = 0.
ε = −N ΔΦ/Δt = −50 × (−0.016)/0.10 = 8.0 V.

7. Capacitance Energy

A 12 μF capacitor is charged to 9.0 V. Charge stored? Energy stored?

Approach

Q = CV = 12×10⁻⁶ × 9 = 1.08×10⁻⁴ C.
U = ½CV² = 0.5 × 12×10⁻⁶ × 81 = 4.9×10⁻⁴ J.
